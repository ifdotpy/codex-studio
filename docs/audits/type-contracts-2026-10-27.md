## Scope and evidence

The source reports used pinned tracked-file inventories and mechanical scans, with focused producer, decoder, owner, and consumer traces where described. Mechanical matches and syntax counts include non-defects; they do not establish that every line or branch was manually read. A typed declaration alone does not validate incoming JSON. Some families explicitly require more producer, historical-format, or lifecycle evidence before a change can be designed. Preserve the semantic, identity, authorization, compatibility, and uncertainty checks identified in each row when evaluating a candidate.

The seven manifests contain 3,009 distinct tracked paths. The seventh report scanned 242 of its 265 inventoried paths. Its vendor provenance comparison found 134 of 134 Layr manifest blobs matching and 37 of 40 Layr Desk blobs matching; the three local test deltas are recorded in the [vendor report](type-contracts-2026-10-27/tooling-vendor.md). Vendor findings are audit-only.

The extension-based inventory identifies 458 source-like paths outside all seven inventories: 388 under the web app (375 in `tests/` and 13 public, configuration or setup files), 61 in the VM guest, 6 in operations, and one each in native-layr, runtime-priority-sys and runtime-proc-sys. These counts classify paths, not behavior or candidate families.

The [path inventory](type-contracts-2026-10-27/inventory.json) compares each report's exact tracked-path manifest with `git ls-tree` at the pinned revision. It also lists source-like paths outside the union by exact path and root, including tests, generated files and scripts. That extension-based gap inventory is not a full semantic source census. An excluded or uncovered path is not evidence of a defect or proof that no boundary exists. Reports may trace callers outside their assigned roots without claiming those roots were censused. The services report also names 20 assigned-away exclusions within its inventoried root; inclusion in a manifest does not mean those files were scanned.

No builds, tests, generators, runtime reads, or live requests were performed for this audit. Proposed witnesses remain unrun. Historical line references and inventories apply only to the pinned commit.
