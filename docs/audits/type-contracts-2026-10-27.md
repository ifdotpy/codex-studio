## Scope and evidence

Mechanical matches and syntax counts include non-defects; they do not establish that every line or branch was manually read. A typed declaration alone does not validate incoming JSON. Some families explicitly require more producer, historical-format, or lifecycle evidence before a change can be designed. Preserve the semantic, identity, authorization, compatibility, and uncertainty checks identified in each row when evaluating a candidate.
